The Bridge Burners and their fellow travelers, sorted into the **ROOT0 specialty
classes**. Wizards are filed by what their magic *does* — a color system, not warrens.
It's an interpretation; `roster.json` is the source of truth, so re-sort freely.

---

## The classes

**Wizards** — by the color of what they do:

| Color | Does | Who wears it |
|-------|------|--------------|
| ⚪ **White** | healing | Mallet |
| ⚫ **Black** | destruction | Bluepearl · Spindle · Fiddler · Hedge |
| 🔴 **Red** | both | Beak |
| 🟣 **Purple** | all + shadow | Tayschrenn *(— and Quick Ben by right; he files himself among the Aristocrats)* |

Then the role classes:

- **Aristocrats** — the brains, the schemers
- **Fixers** — the ones you send
- **Comedy Relief** — deadly, and funny
- **Shadow Games** — the throne's players
- **Economic Stability** — the quiet apocalypse
- **Undying Loyalty** — Tehol's household
- **The Witness** — he will not look away

## The roll

### ⚪ Wizard · White — healing
- **Mallet** — squad cutter; Denul healing keeps the company breathing when the warrens go quiet.

### ⚫ Wizard · Black — destruction
- **Bluepearl** — Mockra and raw force; the blunt instrument of the squad's magic.
- **Spindle** — sapper-mage in the famous hair shirt; destruction by craft.
- **Fiddler** — sapper, no warren — just cussers, sharpers, and nerve. Later Master of the Deck; the moral spine.
- **Hedge** — Fiddler's partner in munitions. Death didn't retire him; he came back to command the *dead* Bridge Burners.

### 🔴 Wizard · Red — both
- **Beak** — the savant. Shields and annihilates with the same hand; one candle of impossible genius.

### 🟣 Wizard · Purple — all + shadow
- **Tayschrenn** — Imperial High Mage; the closest any mortal comes to holding it all.

### 🟡 Aristocrats — the brains
- **Quick Ben** (Ben Adaephon Delat) — twelve souls, every warren, the plan inside the plan inside the plan. *Purple by right; aristocrat by design.*

### Fixers — the ones you send
- **Kalam Mekhar** — the long knives; Quick Ben's other half; the man you send when it simply must be done.

### Comedy Relief — deadly, and funny
- **Gesler & Stormy** — the eternal bickering act; half-immortal, fully lethal.
- **The Marines** — Hellian (drunk), Throatslitter, Deadsmell, and the rest — gallows humor holding the line.

### Shadow Games — the throne's players
- **Cotillion** — the Rope; patron god of assassins; once the man **Dancer**; the blade that possessed Apsalar.
- **Shadowthrone** — Ammanas; the throne of Shadow itself; once the Emperor **Kellanved**.
- **Dancer** — Kellanved's partner; ascended *into* Cotillion — two names, one knife across time.
- **Apsalar** — once "Sorry," a Bridge Burner; possessed by Cotillion into a peerless assassin; now her own avatar → [the apsalar repo](https://github.com/DavidWise01/apsalar).

### Economic Stability — the quiet apocalypse
- **Tehol Beddict** — crashed an empire's economy on purpose, mostly from a rooftop, mostly in a blanket.
- **Bugg** — Tehol's manservant and the engine of the scheme; secretly **Mael**, Elder God of the Sea.

### Undying Loyalty — Tehol's household
- **Brys Beddict** — Tehol's brother; King's Champion; honor that does not bend.
- **Tehol's Household** — the rooftop court, loyal to the last absurd, world-saving scheme.

### The Witness
- **Karsa Orlong** — Toblakai. *"Witness."* He will not look away, and he demands that you do not either. The one who stands and sees.

## Notes

- This sorts Malazan characters (Steven Erikson, *Malazan Book of the Fallen*) into a custom
  scheme. Characters and lore are referenced as inspiration only.
- Quick Ben genuinely spans Purple and Aristocrat; Bugg spans Economic Stability and Undying
  Loyalty; Cotillion/Dancer are one being across an ascension. The overlaps are the point —
  great characters don't fit one box. Filed by their loudest note.
- Edit `roster.json` to re-class anyone. The page and this roll read from it.
